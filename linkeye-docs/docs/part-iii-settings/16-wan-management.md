# 16. WAN Management

WAN Management holds the master records for links, edge devices, and
vendors.

## WAN Links

Add, edit, or delete individual links.

## WAN Edge Devices

Add, edit, or delete devices; view each device's Health Monitor; Pause
Scan when needed.

## Vendors

Add, edit, or delete the vendor records referenced throughout Compliance,
CIO Insights, and Inventory.
