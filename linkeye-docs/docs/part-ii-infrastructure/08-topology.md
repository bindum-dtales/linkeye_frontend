# 8. Topology

Topology renders your infrastructure as a live map of relationships
rather than a flat list — useful for understanding blast radius before
you act on an incident.

## Four Views

| View | Groups the topology by |
|---|---|
| Business Unit | Organizational ownership |
| Site Group | Operational grouping (Stores, Warehouses, HQ, DCs) |
| Zones | Geographic or logical zone |
| Status | Current health state |

Any of the four views supports a deep Site View drilldown. Selecting a
node opens that site's own topology, isolated from the rest of the map.

Topology views are generated from your Sites, Business Unit, Zone, and
Group definitions under Settings → Sites (see [Part III, Chapter 15](../part-iii-settings/15-sites.md)).

If a site isn't appearing where you expect it, confirm its Business
Unit/Zone/Group mapping there first.
