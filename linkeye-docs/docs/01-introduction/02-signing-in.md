# Signing In

LinkEye supports four sign-in methods. All four land on the Overview page
once authenticated.

## Email and Password

1. Enter your registered email and password on the login screen.
    - If your account has access to more than one tenant, select the customer you want to work in.
2. You land on the Overview page for the selected customer.

## OTP Login

1. Enter your registered phone number.
2. Enter the one-time password sent to that number. You land on Overview.

## Google / Microsoft SSO

1. Select Google or Microsoft on the login screen.
2. Complete authentication in the redirected third-party flow. You're returned to LinkEye and land on Overview.

> **Multi-Tenancy**
>
> Every session starts with a customer selection if your account spans
> multiple tenants. You can switch customers at any time from the left
> navigation panel without signing out.
