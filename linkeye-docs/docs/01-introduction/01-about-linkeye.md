# About LinkEye

LinkEye is an agentic AI NetOps platform that unifies telemetry across LAN,
WAN, and Digital Experience (DX) domains. Distributed data collectors sit
across your infrastructure and ingest telemetry over SNMP and APIs. That
telemetry moves through three layers:

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| Network Layer | Devices and links feed live signals into the platform. |
| Data Layer | Structures and contextualizes telemetry; mapping it to sites, vendors, and business units. |
| AI Intelligence Layer | Analyzes behavior, detects anomalies, and activates specialized agents for diagnostics and automation. |

The platform is organized around three functional areas that map directly
to how you'll navigate it day to day:

- **Campus Network:** edge devices, wireless infrastructure, and internal device health.
- **WAN:** links, providers, SLA tracking, and WAN economics.
- **Digital Experience:** the end-to-end path between a site and a business-critical application.

These three areas are reflected directly in the left navigation panel
(Home, Infra, Settings).
