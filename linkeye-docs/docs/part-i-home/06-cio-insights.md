# 6. CIO Insights

CIO Insights translates operational data into the metrics leadership acts
on: SLA compliance, provider accountability, and cost impact. Every
module below filters by time period.

## WAN Economics

Summarizes Total Penalty Cost, with Average Loss by Vendor and Average
Loss by Location. Hover over a segment for details; click it to open the
underlying list of sites. The WAN Economics table lists penalty cost per
month, per circuit ID.

## Availability Dashboard

An executive overview of total downtime elapsed, sites-down count, total
link downtime events, and total SLA breaches. An overview of the sites
with the highest downtime events, downtime % by ISP vendor, and a monthly
link uptime chart (achieved vs. target) is also available for review.

## ISP Metrics

A dashboard (current uptime, goal uptime, difference) with a visual
representation of vendors with the highest downtime. Click a vendor to
open its Vendor Details page with promised uptime and Time-to-Resolution
(TTR) and all other relevant details broken down by site.

## Availability Metrics

A static reference table, filterable by time period only. Useful as a
detailed source-of-truth for all information presented in the
Availability Dashboard.

## Site Metrics

The site-level mirror of ISP Metrics: total downtime elapsed and
site-down count up top, a ring chart of sites with the most downtime
events, and a Site Details page (name, location, downtime events, SLA
breaches, down count, monthly link uptime chart) on click.

## Link Quality

Tracks link degradation rather than outright downtime. Total degradation
events and degrading-link count, with charts for site-wise and ISP-wise
degradation hours. Clicking a chart segment opens the filtered table
(Serial No., Link Vendor, WAN ID, and related fields).

## Bandwidth Trend

Plots actual bandwidth against provisioned speed over time, with a full
entries table beneath the graph — the fastest way to spot under- or
over-provisioned circuits.

## Flapping Links

Total flapping links and total flapping event duration, a flapping
event-count graph, a ring chart of sites with the most flapping links
(click through to site-level detail), and ISP-wise flapping links (click
through to ISP-level detail), all backed by a detailed events table.
